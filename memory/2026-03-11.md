## Business Model Discussion
- JFS exploring surplus proceeds recovery as a business
- Industry standard: 25-35% contingency fee (30% sweet spot)
- Non-attorney companies charge 25-30%, attorneys 33%
- Arizona statute ARS §12-1563: former owner has 2 years to claim surplus after sheriff sale
- Discussed nationwide potential: $4.5M-$18M/year at scale (top 20 states)
- Key insight: **sheriff sale surplus recovery is underserved** compared to tax lien surplus (where 90% of competition is)
- Sheriff sales harder to find/automate = competitive moat
- One competitor spotted: "Arizona Foreclosure Excess Proceeds LLC" in Pima County
- Other foreclosure types worth tracking: mechanic's lien, trustee sale overbids, federal tax lien sales
- HOA sheriff sales = sweet spot (small lien, big property value, big surplus, low competition)
