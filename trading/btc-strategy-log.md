# BTC 20W MA Crossover Strategy — Trade Log

## Strategy Rules
- **Chart:** BTC/USD Weekly
- **Entry:** Weekly candle closes ABOVE the 20W MA (after being below it)
- **Stop:** Below the 20W MA (below the entry candle)
- **Target:** 1:1 RR (same distance as stop, above entry)
- **Leverage:** 2-3x max
- **Risk per trade:** 2% of account

## Position Sizing Formula
`Max loss = Account × 2%`
`Position size = Max loss ÷ % distance to stop`
